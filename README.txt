KASI RAISED — WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html: responsive black-and-white storefront prototype with product grid, size selection, cart, subtotal, and demo checkout form.
- kasi-raised-logo-transparent.png: white KR logo with transparent background, prepared from your uploaded image.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This is a front-end prototype. It does NOT take payment, send orders to you, calculate courier costs, or save customer details.
- The product mockups and product names are placeholders. Replace them with your approved designs, real product photos, sizes, prices, stock/production times, and logo.
- Do not accept real customer orders through this demo until a secure checkout/payment integration is connected.
- Never paste payment secret keys into index.html or any public website code.

FREE-HOSTING START
1. Create a GitHub account at https://github.com/
2. Create a new public repository named kasi-raised-store.
3. Upload index.html and commit the file.
4. In repository Settings -> Pages, enable deployment from the main branch and root folder.
5. GitHub Pages will provide a public URL after deployment. Availability and account requirements may change.

SOUTH AFRICAN PAYMENTS
A practical route to investigate is Ecwid + PayFast. PayFast publishes local card and Instant EFT pricing, and its Ecwid integration instructions require a PayFast merchant account and credentials. Ecwid's available plans/payment integrations can change, so verify the current free-plan limits and whether PayFast can be activated on the plan you choose before committing. The static prototype itself cannot securely integrate PayFast.

References:
- PayFast fees: https://payfast.io/fees/
- PayFast + Ecwid: https://payfast.io/integration/plugins/ecwid/
- Ecwid South Africa payment options: https://support.ecwid.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003054719-Payment-options-for-South-Africa

BEFORE LAUNCH
- Add your existing Kasi Raised logo and approved product images.
- Set accurate prices, sizes, production lead times, shipping fees and local delivery areas.
- Add contact information, returns/refunds, privacy and delivery policies.
- Connect a real ecommerce platform/payment provider; test a low-value transaction and refund flow.
- Confirm business, tax, consumer-protection and POPIA obligations relevant to your situation.
